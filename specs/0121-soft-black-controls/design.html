Xovê, the mark

[XOVE] in a slim viewfinder with a red corner and the record O. Point at the big wordmark (or tab to it): the left corners stay put while the word slides left behind them until the X is gone, and the right corners close in to the O; then the corners settle into the icon, where the icon sits in the logo's own left place; the dot does a quick, surprised double blink; then it all moves back.

Controls (spec 0113)

A white slider, a switch that lights red

Red stays for the brand and what's live; the controls stay quiet. The slider is white, like a video player's. The switch's knob overhangs a slim grey track: grey when off, red when on. Try them.

  • Slider: track 3px, rest #ebebec at 22%, filled part and knob #ffffff; knob 12px, 14px on hover or focus.
  • Switch: track 36×14 #3a3a3d in both states; knob 20px with a soft shadow, #bdbdc0 off, red #ef4b4b on; slides in 180 ms.
  • Both keep a visible focus ring and work with the keyboard (arrows for the slider, space for the switch).

Favicon (spec 0113)

Only the symbol, filling the tab

No tile. White on a dark browser, the dark ground colour on a light one; the red is the same in both. Small sizes fill their square and use heavier strokes and a bigger dot, so the symbol reads from afar.

16 px, real pixels (spec 0113)

What the tab actually shows

The favicon rasterised at 16×16, as a browser does, then enlarged with hard pixels so each one shows. On a dark and on a light tab.

Phone icon (spec 0113)

The dark ground, a bigger symbol

Phones fill transparency with black, so the home-screen icon keeps the dark ground, with the symbol at about 70% of it, so it has room to breathe.
